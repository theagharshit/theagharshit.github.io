## What the brand needed

A D2C apparel brand was running its catalogue, per-size inventory, order fulfilment and customer queries in separate places. The owner wanted one system she could run herself, without a developer.

## How I started

I sat with the owner to learn how the business actually runs day to day, then audited the live store. I documented 8 operational gaps, including no control over what goes live, size and stock data hard-coded into pages, broken category navigation and no order tracking. I sequenced the fixes by their effect on revenue and on customers, not by how easy they were.

## What I am building

- One self-serve system for catalogue, per-size inventory, fulfilment and customer queries.
- Stock that cannot oversell, with every stock movement logged for audit.
- A full status history on every order, so fulfilment is traceable.

In build. The brand has not launched it yet, so it is not named here.
