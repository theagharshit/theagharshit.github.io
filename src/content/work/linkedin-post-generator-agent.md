## What it does

The agent automates the slow parts of writing for LinkedIn: coming up with the idea and developing it into a complete post.

## How it is built

It follows a human-in-the-loop design. The agent does the ideation and drafting, and a person reviews and approves the work before anything is posted, so the voice stays theirs.
