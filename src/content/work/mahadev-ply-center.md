## What the shop needed

A plywood and hardware supplier in Lalitpur, Nepal, wanted customers to browse the range by category, and wanted to keep the catalogue up to date himself, without calling a developer for every change.

## What I built

- Category and sub-category pages, each with its own product form in the admin panel.
- An admin panel where the owner adds and edits products himself.
- An enquiry button on every product that opens a WhatsApp chat with the shop.
- A single admin login on the shop's own domain.
