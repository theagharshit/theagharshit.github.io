## What the client needed

Reporting meant pulling numbers out of Google Analytics by hand and writing them up every time.

## What I built

A scheduled pipeline that pulls traffic and conversion metrics from GA4, works out the week-on-week change and delivers a written summary.

## Result

It removed roughly 80% of the manual reporting effort.
