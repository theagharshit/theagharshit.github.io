## What the team needed

An HR team was running employee records out of spreadsheets. I gathered requirements from the HR users, none of them technical, and built what they described.

## What it does

- Create, view, edit and remove employee records through a full CRUD API.
- A responsive dashboard with a sidebar, stat cards and real-time search and filtering.
- Add and edit forms in animated modals.

## How it is built

An Express and TypeScript backend serves the React frontend through Vite middleware, so the whole app runs as one service in development.
